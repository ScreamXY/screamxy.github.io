Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Pacegrain is a running pace calculator for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, made by Pascal Schrei.

Who is responsible

Pascal Schrei, Switzerland. Contact: screamcode@gmail.com. See the legal notice.

Summary

Pacegrain does not collect, share, or sell personal data. There is no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Data on your device

Your entered paces, speeds, finish times, selected distances, and saved targets are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Pacegrain does not send them to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the app removes them. Like other app data, they may be included in device backups you create with iCloud or a computer. Saved targets are not synchronized between devices.

Network access

Pacegrain works offline. It contacts the network only through Apple's StoreKit when you open the tip jar, which loads tip prices and processes any tip you choose to leave.

Optional tips

Tips are in-app purchases processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Pacegrain never receives your name, Apple Account, or payment details; the developer receives only Apple's aggregated sales reports. Tips don't unlock any features.

Third parties

Pacegrain contains no third-party code, SDKs, analytics, or advertising services.

Children

Pacegrain collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You can ask what data I hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Write to the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
