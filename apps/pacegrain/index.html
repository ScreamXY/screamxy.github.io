Pacegrain

A little race math. A clearer finish line. Pacegrain is a private pace calculator for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

Questions, a wrong-looking result, or an idea? Write to screamcode@gmail.com. For a calculation question, include the pace, speed, or finish time you entered, the distance, and the unit.

Frequently asked

Why is my race time different from the projection?

Finish times assume you hold the same pace for the whole distance. They are pace arithmetic, not predictions of race fitness. Course, weather, and pacing all change the real result.

How exact are the distances?

Pacegrain uses exact values: a marathon is 42.195 km, a half marathon 21.0975 km, and a mile 1.609344 km. Calculations keep full precision; finish times round to seconds and target paces to tenths only for display.

Why doesn't my Apple Watch show my saved targets?

Saved targets stay on the device where you saved them. Pacegrain does not sync between iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

Do tips unlock anything?

No. Tips are optional in-app purchases processed by Apple that simply say thanks.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a saved target from its menu in the Saved tab, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on the device.
