Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Short version: King's Cup collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no King's Cup server.

Who is responsible

Pascal Schrei, Switzerland. Contact: screamcode@gmail.com

Hosting

The app is a static website on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you open it, GitHub receives your IP address and the usual request data to deliver the page. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Fonts from Google

The app loads its two typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser sends your IP address to Google for that request. See Google's Privacy Policy. No other third party is contacted.

Data in your browser

Your edited house rules and the current deck are kept in your browser's local storage, on this device only. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing the site data in your browser deletes them. The app sets no cookies.

Your rights

You can ask what data I hold about you, and ask for correction or deletion. Write to the address above. Since King's Cup stores nothing outside your browser, there is normally nothing to hold.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
