King's Cup

A small companion for the King's Cup card game. Shuffle a deck, draw cards, see the rule for each rank, and edit the rules to your group's house rules. Everything is stored in your browser.

Frequently asked

Where are my house rules saved?

In your browser's local storage, on this device only. They are not synced and never sent anywhere. Clearing site data in your browser removes them, and the default rules come back.

Can I reset the rules?

Yes, in the rules screen. Resetting restores the default rule for every rank.

Does the app work offline?

Once loaded, the game needs no connection. Open it once while online and keep the tab.

Please drink responsibly.

King's Cup is a drinking game. The app is for adults who choose to play it. Water counts as a drink.
