RainScope

Where is it raining around you, and how will that change over the next three hours? RainScope answers on your Apple Watch, with no iPhone app needed.

Questions, a forecast that looks wrong, or an idea? Write to screamcode@gmail.com. For a forecast question, include the date and time, roughly where you were, and what the status at the top of the screen said.

Frequently asked

Is this rain radar?

No. RainScope shows a weather model forecast (DWD ICON-D2, via Open-Meteo), drawn like a radar picture. Every frame, including the past 30 minutes, is a model value, so a shower can arrive a little earlier, later, or further away than shown.

How do I move through time?

Turn the Digital Crown to step from 30 minutes ago to three hours ahead in 15-minute steps, or tap play to animate the forecast. Tap the status at the top for the colour legend, the time of the last update, and Refresh.

Where does RainScope work?

It is made for Central Europe: ICON-D2 covers Switzerland, Germany, Austria and their neighbours at about 2 km. Elsewhere RainScope uses a coarser global model whose 15-minute steps are interpolated from hourly data. The status sheet names the model you are seeing.

Does it need my iPhone?

No. RainScope is an Apple Watch app only and needs watchOS 26 or later. It loads the forecast over the watch's own connection: Wi-Fi, cellular, or the paired iPhone nearby.

Why does it say “Location Access Off”?

RainScope needs your location while you use it. On the watch, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services and allow RainScope to use your location while using the app. It never uses your location in the background.

What does “Not Your Area” mean?

You have moved more than 2.5 km since the forecast on screen was loaded, and the forecast for where you are now could not be loaded yet. The old area stays on screen until it can.

The forecast seems old.

RainScope reuses a forecast for 30 minutes, or until you move 2.5 km, so it opens quickly and spares the battery. For a new one, tap the status at the top and choose Refresh.

Why is the map missing?

The map comes from Apple Maps and is kept only while the app runs. If the watch has no connection after a restart, RainScope shows the last forecast over a dark background with 10 and 20 km rings instead.

Why does it say “Usage Limit Reached”?

Open-Meteo's free service limits how often it can be asked for a forecast. RainScope waits until the time it shows, then loads again.

How do I delete my data?

Delete RainScope from your watch to remove everything it stored. Turning off its location access also deletes its saved position. See the privacy policy.
