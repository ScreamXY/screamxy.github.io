Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

RainScope is a precipitation forecast for Apple Watch, made by Pascal Schrei.

Who is responsible

Pascal Schrei, Switzerland. Contact: screamcode@gmail.com. See the legal notice.

Summary

RainScope uses your location to load the forecast around you. It sends your position to the weather service Open-Meteo and, for the map, to Apple. There is no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking, and none of your data reaches the developer.

Your location

RainScope asks for your location only while you use it. When it needs a new forecast, it takes one position fix and then switches location off again. It does not track you and never uses your location in the background.

Weather data from Open-Meteo

To load a forecast, your watch sends the coordinates of 81 points in a 40 × 40 km area centred on your position directly to Open-Meteo (OpenMeteo GmbH, Switzerland). The coordinates are precise to about 10 metres. Like any web server, Open-Meteo also receives your watch's IP address. No name, account, or device identifier is sent. According to Open-Meteo's terms, its server logs may contain coordinates and IP addresses and are deleted after 90 days.

Map from Apple

The map behind the forecast comes from Apple Maps, which receives the area it draws. Apple's Privacy Policy applies.

Data on your watch

RainScope stores two things on your watch: your last position, so it can still show a forecast when the GPS is slow, and the last forecast with the area it covers, so it opens quickly and works offline. If you turn off RainScope's location access, it deletes the stored position; the last forecast stays until a new one replaces it. Deleting the app removes both. Like other app data, they may be included in backups of your watch.

Third parties

RainScope contains no third-party code, SDKs, analytics, or advertising services. Open-Meteo and Apple Maps are the only services it contacts.

Children

RainScope handles everyone's data the same way, including children's, and the developer receives none of it.

Your rights

You can ask what data I hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Write to the address above.
